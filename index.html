02 / AlignmentEvaluate

Feedback with intent.

Useful reward design considers not only correctness, but consistency, constraints and the quality of behavior.

02 / Training signals

Signals shape behavior.

A reward is a proxy for what we value. Better learning depends on making that proxy meaningful, stable and hard to game.

↗

Outcome signals

Use verifiable results where possible: correctness, task completion and measurable outcomes.

⌁

Process signals

Inspect how a solution is reached, especially when a final answer alone hides important failure modes.

◎

Preference signals

Use human or model feedback carefully, with attention to disagreement, bias and reward over-optimization.

03 / The learning loop

Build. Measure. Refine.

A compact mental model for understanding how feedback-driven training can improve a model over repeated iterations.

“The goal is not to optimize a score in isolation. It is to learn behaviors that generalize.”

A guiding principle for reward design and evaluation.

STEP 01

Generate

Sample responses and explore multiple possible strategies for a task.

STEP 02

Evaluate

Compare outcomes against meaningful criteria and identify reliable signals.

STEP 03

Update

Use feedback to update behavior, then test whether gains persist beyond the training set.
